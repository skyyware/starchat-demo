## Privacy

The operator above is responsible for personal data processing on chat.stage.dev.
Use the same contact address for privacy enquiries.

### Your conversation

You can read the guides without an account or using the chat. When you submit
a question, we send it and a limited part of your conversation to OpenAI with
selected public sources to generate and check the answer. The connection uses
Codex through the operator's existing ChatGPT account. Do not enter passwords
or confidential information. Answers and code can contain errors.

Your browser saves the conversation on this device until you choose **New
conversation** or clear the website's data. A storage or deletion failure is
shown in the interface. The browser may also remove data under its own settings.
The application keeps no server-side conversation archive and does not write
questions or answers to its application logs.

When you first visit the new chat address, your browser can copy an existing
Stage conversation from stage.dev. This transfer stays within your browser,
does not replace an existing conversation here, and leaves the old copy in place.

Deleting browser data does not delete information already sent to OpenAI.
OpenAI's processing, retention, and international transfers follow its applicable
service terms and data controls. See [OpenAI's privacy policy](https://openai.com/policies/eu-privacy-policy/)
and [Codex data controls](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/11369540-using-codex-with-your-chatgpt-plan).

Question links can contain text in the address. The browser removes it after
loading an editable draft; no model request happens until you submit. The
original address still reaches the server and may pass through other
infrastructure. Keep personal and confidential information out of these links.

### Website operation

The public chat sets no cookies and uses no advertising or analytics services.
Browsers store the conversation to provide the chat you request. Shared design files and fonts load from stage.dev, operated by the same
provider. Other application assets are hosted here. External sites receive a request when you open their links.

To deliver and protect the website, the server logs IP address, time, HTTP
method, path without query parameters, protocol version, status, and response
size. Access logs omit User-Agent and Referrer. Error logs may include technical
errors and request details. These logs rotate daily, retaining the current file
and up to 14 daily archives per log. Older archives are removed at the next
rotation. The operator and hosting service providers access them as needed.

Delivering the website and keeping security logs serve our legitimate interest
in operating and protecting it under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. Browser storage
supports the requested conversation, not tracking across websites.

Article 6(1)(b) GDPR applies to contract-related enquiries; otherwise Article
6(1)(f) applies to our interest in responding. We retain these data for the
enquiry and any applicable legal retention duties.

Where the legal conditions apply, you may request access, correction, deletion,
restriction, and data portability. You may object to processing based on
legitimate interests for reasons arising from your situation. You may complain
to a supervisory authority, including the State Commissioner for Data Protection

The chat makes no decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you.
